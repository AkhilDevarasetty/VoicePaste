How it works

Three steps. Zero friction.

No window to open. No mode to switch. Your hands stay on the keyboard.

01 — HOLD

Hold Right Option

Anywhere, in any app. No dock bounce, no modal, no context switch.

⌥ Right Option
02 — SPEAK

Speak naturally

A pill overlay confirms recording. Talk for a second or a minute — pauses are handled.

03 — PASTE

Release. Text appears.

Transcribed locally and pasted at your cursor. Whatever app, whatever field.

Features

Built the way it should be built.

Private by default, useful everywhere, polished when you want it.

Local-first

100% on-device transcription.

VoicePaste runs right on your Mac. Your voice doesn't touch a server. Works fully offline, wherever you are.

Privacy

No cloud. Ever.

Simple setup

Free forever

Universal
